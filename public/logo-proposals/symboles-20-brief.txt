Use case: logo-brand. Create one professional logo exploration contact sheet for RIFIM, a radiology and musculoskeletal imaging app. Landscape composition, exactly FIVE columns and TWO rows of large standalone logo EMBLEMS, clean white background, equal generous margins, one original symbol in each cell. These are brand marks, not text logos or font studies. Flat vector-like geometry, strong carefully composed silhouettes, sophisticated negative space, excellent optical balance. Mostly navy, cobalt, burgundy, charcoal with restrained ochre or terracotta accents. Absolutely NO green, gradients, shadows, bevels, texture, 3D render, letters, words, monograms, medical crosses, ECG or caduceus. Only small gray numbers below the symbols. Distinct design vocabulary for every symbol: not ten modifications of one design. Anatomy is an inspiration for identity, not a detailed medical drawing. No old common stock medical logos. Each must look like a refined original brand emblem.
